The simple explainer

Four kinds of private-aviation business

Understanding these categories helps you determine who holds legal responsibility for your flight, who manages your payments, and who coordinates contingencies.

​
​
Type 01

Aircraft operator

The certified air carrier holding an NCAA Air Operator Certificate (AOC), responsible for flight operations, crew compliance, maintenance, and direct safety oversight.

​
Type 02

Integrated aviation services

FBO operators and maintenance facilities that may also provide aircraft management, hangarage, and concierge charter services under various partner arrangements.

​
Type 03

Charter broker

An intermediary that sources aircraft options from certified operators, negotiates pricing, and coordinates passenger arrangements on behalf of clients.

​
Type 04

Digital aviation ecosystem

Digital dispatch layers providing route calculators, airport databases, and mission coordination workflows while partnering with certified third-party operators for flights.

​
Access Model

Jet cards & fractional ownership

Structured access models allowing frequent flyers to purchase hourly blocks or fractional aircraft shares subject to specific fleet availability terms.

​
Safety Principle

Verification over branding

A sleek website or digital portal does not replace regulatory certification. Always examine the operating carrier's credentials and written terms.

Frequently asked questions

Private aviation in Nigeria: quick answers

​
Is a private jet broker the same as an aircraft operator? ↓

No. Brokers arrange charters, whereas operators hold the NCAA Air Operator Certificate and fly the aircraft.

​
What role does the NCAA play? ↓

The Nigerian Civil Aviation Authority regulates civil aviation safety, licensing, and commercial air transport operations in Nigeria.

​
Does an instant online estimate constitute a confirmed booking? ↓

No. Online estimates are planning figures. Final confirmation requires verified aircraft availability, permit approvals, and signed agreements.
